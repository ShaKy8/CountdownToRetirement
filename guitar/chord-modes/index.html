Which mode fits this chord?

Chord-to-Mode Matcher

A mode fits a chord when the mode contains every note of the chord. Think of the chord as a list of required parameters: the mode has to supply all of them, or something clashes.

3 + 7

Read the 3rd and the 7th. The 3rd picks the family: a 3 means a major mode, a ♭3 a minor one. The 7th narrows it: over a major chord, a 7 leaves Ionian or Lydian and a ♭7 leaves Mixolydian. The root and 5th are in nearly every mode, so they rarely decide anything.
